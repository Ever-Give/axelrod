Chapter 1: Multiple Choice Housing

When Brook opened the notification for an email from Omorashi University on his phone, he was expecting some more helpful information or another automated “fill this form out” thing before the year began, perhaps a thing to sign up for clubs or sports teams, not whatever this was. The more he read, the worse it got. Well, at least it started with an apology, and a sincere sounding one at that. Apparently, someone in the team that assigned students to specific dorms had seen the name Brook, read it as “Brooke”, and just assumed the person in question had to be female.

With only one month left before the college year began, the standard dorms had already been assigned to 100% capacity, so there was no room for him to be moved into the male only building. In a college where males and females were traditionally separated, he wondered if this would mean he wasn’t going to get college housing. He sent a reply, basically saying that he is very disappointed to hear this and explaining that he needs to know what happens next.

The reply came late the next day. There were only three openings left, and as a courtesy to him for the misunderstanding caused by the admin team, they would let him have his pick of which option he wanted to take. The options were a single apartment above a pizza shop, and while the college tuition covered rent for him, he would have to pay his own utilities. Not only that, but there was no cross walk at the intersections between the restaurant side of omo street and the college side of omo street without walking almost half a mile down towards the older cultural restaurants where the red lights had sensors instead of timers and the intersections actually had crosswalks. Also there was no bus connecting the apartment unit to the college without having to walk just as far but in the opposite direction. He could do it if he had to, but he’d rather look at the other three options before committing to this one.

The second option was a condo adjacent to the college dorms in an old building notorious for utility failures and electrical issues, but at least it had dedicated crosswalks in all directions and there were bus stops in all directions within a five minute walk from the main entrance. His tuition paid for everything including utilities, but if the reviews were anything to go by, then he should expect to be without power for at least a week every two months. He could survive without power every now and then if the third option was no good. He considered this option as he clicked on the link to the third option. It was one of those double dorm setups where two roommates shared a dorm, but it was a joined double, meaning four roommates total.

Looking at the area on Google Maps as well as the black and white map provided by Omorashi University, it looked like each of these buildings were completely separate and each had covered parking as well as EV charging. While his plug in hybrid didn’t technically *need* to be plugged in and would drive like a normal hybrid if he just put gas in it like any other car, he did have to admit that waking up to a fully charged battery and being able to drive in the electric only mode would be nice.

If he was lucky, he might be able to stretch it far enough to only have to go to a gas station once every three weeks if his understanding of Google Maps and his car’s estimated efficiency was correct. At the bottom, there was an explanation of an asterisk that stated that EV charging was free so long as you didn’t stay plugged in for more than four hours at a time so that other people could use the charger as well. He could definitely work with that. His car didn’t take that long to charge anyway.

He replied and confirmed that he would like to go with the third option: the Doubled Up Double Dorms. Whoever came up with that name truly lacked creativity. He chuckled to himself as he wondered if it was the same person that messed up his original housing. Not even a full day later, he received another email with the exact address and building number he would be staying in and a notification that he could move in immediately if he liked. At the bottom, it listed really low res pictures of what looked like an outdoor pool area, a pavilion filled with picnic tables that was surrounded by charcoal grills and tiki torches, and a few pictures of a central building that included laundry facilities and a pet bathing area.

Now that he thought about it, none of the info he had received explicitly stated whether pets were allowed or not. He had just assumed it was a pet free area like most college dorms were. With all this information, all that was really left unanswered was who his roommates would be. Probably someone loud and greasy, someone so absorbed in their studies that they didn’t go to any parties and all, and someone who partied all day and all night and somehow didn’t drop out. Yeah, that seemed to sum up what he expected to see when he pulled up.

When he arrived a few days later, he wasn’t sure he was at the right place when he was the only car in the four covered spaces beside Building A. However, as he checked his email one more time, he was sure he was at the right spot. The address and building number were both correct. This had to be it. As he got out of his car and walked up the small flight of stairs to the sidewalk that went to the front door, he could see through the window where a slender young lady lay on the couch watching TV while another was making something in the kitchen… Well, the chubbier girl in the kitchen seemed to be making more smoke than food, but maybe it was supposed to be that way?

The third girl came into view only for a moment, appearing from behind a bedroom door and sprinting at mach three before ripping open a door and disappearing. Probably the bathroom. He wasn’t close enough to see real detail, but it looked like there was some diversity here in terms of skin color and body weight… Wait… They were all girls! Had he been accidentally assigned to the girl’s dorms even after all the trouble with the housing people had seemingly been resolved?
