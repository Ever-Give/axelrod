I just had wildest omo dream I could possibly have, and it included everything I love~

What intrigued me the most is that everyone here had to wear shiny, reflective spandex bodysuits! Both boys and girls. And if you felt cold - you could put something tight on top of your bodysuit!

You probably guessed it already. I went into a dressing room and there was an entire set to pick from! Bodysuits, leotards, coats, speedos... I picked a shiny silver spandex bodysuit and went to get dressed but it felt a bit cold. So I also picked a shiny green leotard with short sleeves and pulled it over my bodysuit. It felt so good! It was almost like I was having a full body hug!

I went to have fun skating. The more I moved, the better my spandex felt. I rolled around different rooms. There were racing rings, dancing rooms, big rooms for free running, and everyone in these rooms wore some kind of tight and shiny attire! Some were just wearing bodysuits, some were wearing suits and leotards like me, some were wearing tight coats on top of their suits~

I had some fun but eventually the nature called. I was tempted to wait until I was about to burst and then let a waterfall into my suit but... Not in front of everyone! xP

And so I went into nearby bathroom. And do you know how it worked?

You lock your skates into specialized slots, stand above a drain on the floor... And you guessed it, you let go right into your shiny attire!

But I felt a bit too shy to do that at the moment so I was about to leave... When some guy rushed in and barely had time to lock his skates before he let loose in front of me! He was wearing an attire similar to mine, except his leotard was blue and had no sleeves. While he was wetting himself his eyes rolled back as he sagged down slightly, visibly relieved.

It was one kind of a show, but eventually he finished peeing and looked at me, saying that I better let loose here and not somewhere else, or I will have to spend an hour in "Stained jail". I asked him what it is and he led me across the building. While we were moving, I could see last drops of pee dribble out of his suit and onto the floor~

We reached the jail and I saw what it was. Folks who peed themselves NOT in the bathroom had to drink a large glass of water, pick a spot and stand still there for an hour! Yes, standing still in the roller skates, wearing tight and shiny artire while your urge grows~

Best part? If you peed yourself during this time - the floor becomes slippery and the difficulty of keeping your balance rises! And if you fail, fall onto your knees or your butt, the timer resets. And you cannot change your spot, so good luck standing in that pee puddle~

Gosh this dream was truly wild. I never had dreams like this before! So detailed~
