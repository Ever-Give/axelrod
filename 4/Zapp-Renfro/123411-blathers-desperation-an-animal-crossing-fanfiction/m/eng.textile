Blather's desperation (An Animal Crossing Fanfiction)

Author: Zapp Renfro

*000*2026-04-15 17:34

The museum is quiet this morning, a hallowed stillness that Blathers cherishes. He shuffles from the main hall, his wings tucked close to his body, and pushes through the doors of The Roost. The familiar, rich aroma of freshly ground coffee beans hangs in the air, a warm blanket against the morning's chill.

"Brewster, my good friend," Blathers hoots softly, approaching the counter. "A coffee, if you please. The usual."

Brewster nods, a silent, efficient figure as he moves behind his gleaming coffee machine. The hiss of steam and the rhythmic grinding of beans are the only sounds. A moment later, a steaming cup of black coffee is placed on the counter. Blathers pays him a few bells, wraps his wings around the warm ceramic, and takes a long, appreciative sip. The bitter, dark liquid is a comfort, a jolt of warmth that spreads through his feathered chest.

He finishes the cup with a few more quick gulps, the hot coffee settling pleasantly in his stomach. Thanking Brewster with another hoot, he heads back to his post. He takes his position just inside the grand entrance, standing beneath the high, arched ceiling. His job is to greet visitors, to offer facts and enthusiasm for the treasures housed within. But on a day like this, with the sun barely up and the island still stirring, the lobby is empty.

The warmth of the day, combined with the profound silence of the museum, begins to work its familiar magic. His eyelids grow heavy. He shifts from one foot to the other, trying to stay alert, but the steady, rhythmic beat of his own heart is a lullaby. His head bobs once, then twice, and soon, he's slumped against the podium, fast asleep in a puddle of afternoon sunshine.

When he wakes with a start, the sun has moved, casting long, dramatic shadows across the polished floor. He blinks, disoriented, and that's when he feels it. A deep, insistent pressure low in his abdomen. The coffee, he realizes with a jolt of panic, has made its way through his system. He needs to go. Now.

He straightens up, intending to make a beeline for the staff restroom, but the moment he stands fully, the pressure intensifies tenfold. A dull ache radiates through his lower belly, and he has to clench the muscles he hadn't realized he was paying attention to. He lets out a quiet, strained hoot, squeezing his thighs together.

Just as he takes a tentative, shuffling step forward, the museum doors swing open with a cheerful chime. In walks a villager, a chipper little dog named Goldie, clutching a fossil in her paws.

"Oh, Blathers! You're awake!" she yips, trotting right up to him. "I was hoping you could tell me about this!"

Blathers' feathers puff out in a mixture of surprise and distress. He's trapped. To abandon his post now would be rude. To stay…oh, to stay is torture. He plasters a strained smile on his face, one that doesn't quite reach his wide, panicked eyes.

"Why, of course, Goldie! Hoo! Always a delight to see a new discovery!" he says, his voice an octave higher than usual.

He can't focus. The pressure is a living thing inside him, a heavy, hot stone that demands release. He shifts his weight, bouncing slightly from the ball of his left foot to the ball of his right. A subtle, frantic little dance.

"This appears to be…ah…a humerus, from a large mammal," Blathers begins, trying to keep the tremor out of his voice. "The cortical bone thickness suggests a creature of significant heft, perhaps a relative of the modern day elephant. Hoo!"

He crosses his legs, one wing subtly pressing against his crotch. The movement is fleeting but desperate. Goldie tilts her head, her floppy ears flopping to one side. "Are you okay, Blathers? You're kinda…wiggling."

"Fascinating, isn't it?" he barrels on, ignoring her question completely, a drop of sweat beading on his brow. "The fossilization process itself is simply marvelous, you see! Minerals from the surrounding sediment gradually replace the organic material in the bone, cell by cell, over millions of years! Hoo! It preserves a snapshot of life from a bygone era!"

His leg begins to jiggle uncontrollably. He has to press both wings down against the podium now, leaning his weight on his arms to keep from grabbing himself outright. His bladder spasms, a sharp, warning cramp that makes him gasp audibly.

"Hoo…excuse me," he squeaks, squeezing his eyes shut for a second. "As I was saying…the striations on the bone here indicate…indicate powerful muscle attachments. This creature was…was…a powerful digger!"

Goldie is no longer looking at the fossil. Her gaze is fixed on Blathers, her expression a mix of concern and bewilderment. He's doing a full blown dance now, a subtle but constant shifting of his hips, a frantic jiggle of his bent knees. His face is flushed, and he keeps letting out little pained hoots between sentences.

"Blathers…maybe you should…?" she starts to say, but he cuts her off, desperate to finish and be done.

"The sheer age of it is…is simply…simply…oh, hoo…" He trails off, bouncing on the balls of his feet, a wave of intense pressure nearly bowling him over. He clenches with everything he has, a full-body shudder of effort.

Finally, Goldie just nods slowly, backing away. "Okay…well…thanks for the info," she says, her eyes wide. "I'll just…leave this here." She places the fossil on the podium and scurries out of the museum, casting one last, strange look over her shoulder at the writhing owl.

The moment the doors swing shut, Blathers gasps. He can't hold it any longer. His bladder is screaming, a pulsing, hot agony. He abandons all pretense, both wings flying down to clamp between his legs. He hunches over, letting out a low, groaning hoot of pure desperation. He's going to burst. He has to get to the bathroom right now.

Blathers takes a single, hobbling step away from the podium. Then another. He's almost to the hallway, the sign for the restroom a beacon of hope, when the doors chime again.

"Blathers! Big brother!" a cheerful, chirpy voice calls out.

Celeste. His little sister.

He freezes, his body rigid with panic. He can't move. He can't speak. He can only stand there, trembling on the precipice of an accident, as his sister trots over to him, her starry-patterned wings flapping happily.

"I was just passing by and thought I'd pop in! Are you alright? You look a bit…flustered."

He tries to answer, to warn her, but all that comes out is a choked hoot. The sudden shock of her arrival is the final straw. He feels the dam inside him break. A hot spurt of wetness escapes him, soaking into the feathers between his legs. He gasps, a mortified squeak, and instinctively squeezes harder, but it's too late. The floodgates have opened.

A quiet hiss fills the silence of the museum. His legs go weak, and he stands paralyzed, as the steady stream trickles down his legs, dripping onto the polished floor to form a small, humiliating puddle at his feet. The relief is immediate and absolute, but it's overshadowed by a tidal wave of shame.

Celeste's cheerful expression melts away, replaced by wide eyed shock. Her gaze travels from his horrified, tear filled eyes down to the rapidly growing wet spot, and then to the puddle on the floor. The silence is deafening.

"Blathers…?" she whispers, her voice full of dawning, horrified understanding.

Blathers just stands there, trembling, soaked in his own urine, the evidence of his complete and utter failure trickling onto the floor as his sister watches. He has never been so humiliated in his entire life.
